Pet Routine Tracker

Everyday pet care, remembered.

Feeding, water, walks, litter, grooming, play and training, for every pet in the house, in one calm place on your iPhone.

Free. No account. Works offline.

What it does

Open the app and you can see what each pet needs today, what has already been done, and what is coming next. Logging a routine takes one tap, and you can undo it if you tap the wrong one.

Routines repeat every day, on days you choose, or every few days. Give one a set time and an optional reminder, or leave it as “any time”. Pause a routine when life changes, without losing its history.

Your data stays on your iPhone

There is no account, no cloud and no tracking. The app makes no network requests at all. It works entirely offline, including in aeroplane mode. You can export a readable backup whenever you like, or delete everything from Settings.
