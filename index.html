A calm pet care app for the whole household

Open the app and you can see what each pet needs today, what has already been done, and what is coming next. Logging a routine takes one tap, and you can undo it if you tap the wrong one.

Add as many pets as you like, each with an illustrated avatar: cats, dogs, rabbits, fish, reptiles and more. Feeding, fresh water, walks, litter or habitat cleaning, grooming, play and training are built in, and you can add your own routines for anything else.

Dog and cat feeding reminders, on your schedule

Routines repeat every day, on days you choose, or every few days. Give one a set time and an optional reminder, or leave it as “any time”. Pause a routine when life changes, without losing its history.

Skip a routine with a note when plans change, and fix the time or add a note later. History can be filtered by pet, routine type and date, so you can check when the cat was last fed or the hutch last cleaned.

Your data stays on your iPhone

There is no account, no cloud and no tracking. The app makes no network requests at all. It works entirely offline, including in aeroplane mode. You can export a readable backup whenever you like, or delete everything from Settings.

Common questions about Pet Routine Tracker

Is there a free app to track pet feeding?

Yes. Pet Routine Tracker is free on iPhone, with no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads. Add a feeding routine for each pet, set it for every day, chosen weekdays or every few days, and log each meal with one tap.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The app makes no network requests and needs no account, so it works anywhere, including in aeroplane mode. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone.

Can I track several pets?

Yes. Add as many pets as you like, each with its own illustrated avatar and routines. The Today screen shows what is due now, later and done across the whole household, and History can be filtered by pet.

Can it remind me to give my pet medicine?

You can add a custom routine, such as “Give tablet”, with a set time and a reminder, then log each dose with one tap. The app only keeps track of the routines you set up. It does not give medical or dosage advice, so always follow your vet’s instructions.

Does it share between family phones?

Not yet. Everything stays on one iPhone, and there is no syncing or sharing between devices.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your pets, routines and history are stored only on your iPhone, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to export a backup yourself. The privacy policy has the detail.

Pet Routine Tracker runs on iPhone with iOS 15 or later. It supports VoiceOver, larger text, Reduce Motion, light and dark mode, and 12 or 24-hour time.
